Questions

Before you order.

Can't find your answer? Message us on Telegram at @lilac234.

How do I place an order?

Call or text us on 0953 911 250, message @lilac234 on Telegram, or chat with us on WhatsApp. Tell us the occasion, your budget, preferred colours and the date — or send a photo from our collection — and we'll design it for you.

Can I order from outside Ethiopia?

Yes. Send us a Telegram message with the recipient's name, phone number and delivery details, and we'll confirm the design and payment options with you.

Do you deliver?

We deliver within Addis Ababa. Delivery fees depend on the location — ask us when you order. You're also welcome to pick up from our shop in Kazanchis.

Can I add chocolates, a cake or a teddy?

Of course. We regularly pair flowers with chocolates, cakes, teddy bears, balloons and our custom Jar of Notes. Tell us what you have in mind.

How far in advance should I order?

For bouquets and gifts, a day's notice is ideal, though we can often help with same-day requests. For weddings and events, please contact us as early as possible to reserve the date.

Can you match my colours or theme?

Absolutely. Every arrangement is made to order — share a photo, a colour palette or an idea and we'll design around it.
